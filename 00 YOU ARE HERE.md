# YOU ARE HERE — The Vault at a Glance

> **"A knowledge base is a conversation with your future self."**
> This vault helps you see every conversation you've ever had with every model in OpenCode.

## What Is This Place?

This is your **OpenCode Obsidian Vault**. It automatically organizes all your chats with every model you’ve ever used. If you’re switching between 3 different models for the same project, this vault keeps them all tracked and shows you how they connect.

## How It Works
1. **You chat** with a model in OpenCode.
2. **The chat is saved** into the `Input/` folder.
3. **This dashboard auto-uppliers** a summary to the correct **Model Folder**.
4. **The Graph View** shows you which models are related to which topics.

## The Model Folders
- `Models/`: Where every model you’ve ever used lives.
- `Input/`: Every new conversation starts here.
- `Graphs/`: Visual maps of how models and topics connect.
- `Daily/`: High-level daily thoughts and overviews.
